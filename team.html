Akachukwu D. Obi, Ph.D.

Principal Investigator

Dr. Obi earned his Ph.D. in Chemistry from the University of Virginia (2022) under Prof. Robert J. Gilliard Jr., studying energy-relevant molecular magnesium and bismuth complexes. He subsequently led carbon capture research as a Postdoctoral Impact Fellow at the MIT Climate and Sustainability Consortium, followed by a Postdoctoral Associate position with Prof. Alex Radosevich at MIT Chemistry. In July 2026, he started his independent career as an Assistant Professor of Integrated Sciences: Chemistry of Materials at Claremont Mckenna College.
