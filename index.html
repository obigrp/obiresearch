How to Join

We welcome motivated graduate students, undergraduates, and postdoctoral researchers interested in main-group chemistry, carbon capture, and sustainable materials design.

  • Graduate Students: Apply through the MIT Chemistry graduate program and mention your interest in our research areas.
  • Undergraduates: Reach out directly by email with your CV and research interests.
  • Postdocs: Email with CV, cover letter, and names of three references.
